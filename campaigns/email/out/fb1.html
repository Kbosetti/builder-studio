Building on your land

Your land can be your down payment

You already own the hardest part of building a custom home. Most landowners do not realize how far along that puts them.

Most builders send you to a bank for a construction loan before anyone turns a shovel. We do not. Mitchell self-funds every build, so the land you hold counts toward the home, with zero down, zero closing costs and no construction loan.

That means one closing instead of two, no draw period interest while the house goes up, and no bank deciding whether your land counts.
