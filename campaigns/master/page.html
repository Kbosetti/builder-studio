Brittany's idea

My Mitchell Story

For the people who already built with Mitchell. Past homeowners share a short phone video about the home they love, and every entrant gets a professional photo session at home, paid for by Mitchell and timed so the photos arrive for holiday cards.

How it runs

  • It kicks off at Homeowner Appreciation Night, Tuesday, November 17, at all five Design Centers.
  • Homeowners who enter by November 29 can book their session between November 18 and December 5, with edited photos back within five business days, in time for holiday cards.
  • Five finalists, one from each Design Center region, receive a framed painting of their own Mitchell home, and one grand prize winner also receives $2,500.
  • Entries close December 13, and the winner is announced December 18.
  • Past homeowners are their own list, so none of this adds to what buyers in the database receive.
