    For leads who have not answered yet

    When a lead goes quiet for seven days, Home Builder Studio starts a 60-day sequence on its own: personal notes from their consultant, designed emails and short texts, until they reply or book. The moment they answer, it stops and their consultant takes over.

    Brittany's idea. Mitchell homeowners tell the story of their home in a short phone video. It starts with Homeowner Appreciation Night at the Design Centers on November 17. Every entrant gets a professional photo session at home, timed so the photos arrive for holiday cards, five finalists receive a framed painting of their own Mitchell home, and one grand prize winner also receives $2,500.
