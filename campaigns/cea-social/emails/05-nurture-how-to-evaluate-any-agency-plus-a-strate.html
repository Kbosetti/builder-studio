Many builders we meet have been burned by an agency before. If that is you, I understand why you are careful.

So here are the questions I would ask any agency, including CEA:

  1. How many hours will a real person spend on my account each month?
  2. What will you fix in the first 30 days?
  3. How do you track a lead from click to closing?
  4. What does my brand look like in AI search today?
  5. How do you filter out junk leads?
  6. How will you use my existing photos and video?
  7. When should I expect results?

Listen for specific answers, not promises. An honest agency separates quick ad fixes from longer AI visibility work.
