The rules

What keeps it from feeling like too much

Two emails a week at most.Tuesday goes to the full list. Thursday goes only to contacts who opened or clicked in the last 90 days, plus every lead since September 1. Everyone else hears from us once a week.
One text a week at most.Only to contacts with text consent, never on the same day as their email, and only to people who did not open Tuesday's email.
A person follows every signal.Quiz takers and form fills hear from someone within 15 minutes. Anyone who clicks hears from a consultant within one business day, and skips that week's marketing text.
Conversations come first.Anyone booked, under contract, or talking with a consultant comes off the campaign emails and texts, so they only hear from their consultant.
Deadline week is the one heavy week.October 26 to 31 carries two Design Dollars emails, a text, a personal email and a personal text before the October 31 reserve by date. If Mitchell approves a November date, the same pattern repeats the week of November 23.
Holds slide, nothing else moves.A piece marked Hold goes out in the next open slot of its kind once its question is answered. The rest of the calendar stays as it is.
