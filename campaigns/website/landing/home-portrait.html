The Home Portrait in under 30 seconds

Questions

Before you begin.

Do I need to own land?

No. Buyers who own land, share family land, or have no land yet are all welcome, and no land yet is a good answer on the quiz. Your portrait starts from where you are.

How long does it take?

About 90 seconds. There are eight questions: the home first, the land in the middle, and your timing last. Your portrait is ready the moment you finish.

What happens after?

Your portrait ends with one next step: a Design Studio visit with a New Home Consultant. You decide if and when.

You can also have the full portrait sent to your email to read at your own pace.

Is there any cost?

No. The quiz and your Home Portrait cost nothing. If you decide to build, SimplyMitchell means zero down and zero closing costs, and the $150 reservation deposit is all Mitchell receives until closing.
