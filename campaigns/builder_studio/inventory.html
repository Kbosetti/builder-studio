Already working in the account

These have run since September. They create records for every lead, so they are the one part of our work that is live today.

  • The Home Portrait quiz at mitchellhomesliving.com/portrait. Every finished quiz creates or updates the contact, fills the quiz fields below, and tags it home portrait quiz, plus the persona, land status, division, region, timeline and selections. It adds qualified lead when the buyer owns land and plans to build within a year. It also adds a quiz results note, sends the portrait email with the PDF, and creates or updates the opportunity in the division's pipeline.
  • The landing page forms (Your Land, Two Ways to Build, the Land Savings Calculator, the Dreamer pages and both Design Dollars pages). Leads come in tagged landing page lead plus the page name, with an opportunity in the online sales counselor pipeline.
  • Booking buttons. "Visit a Design Studio" on the quiz checks the division's Design Studio Visit calendar on every click. The calendars are switched off right now, so buyers land on that studio's page on mitchellhomesinc.com instead. Turning a calendar on with open times switches the button to the booking page, with no other change.
