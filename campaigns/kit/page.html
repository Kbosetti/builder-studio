What is inside, and what to settle first

In this kit

    Before anything goes out

    1. Sending domain. Builder Studio emails still go out from the platform's default address. Add a Mitchell sending domain before a 30,000 contact send, or deliverability will suffer.
    2. Unsubscribe link. The emails use the Builder Studio {{unsubscribe}} tag. Send one test first. If the account's automatic footer link is on, delete the tag so there is only one. Sending from Lasso instead? Swap in Lasso's tag.
    3. The two August promotion pages still headline $10k and $15k off, which Design Dollars replaced on September 1. Those ads are still running.
    4. The Dreamer pages promise a price locked from day one, which the pricing rule does not allow. The Dreamer email and the posts that link there are on hold until the line comes off.
    5. The /math and /calculator pages still carry Brittany's hold and unconfirmed comparison figures. The emails now point landowners to /land instead; the Transitioner email waits on legal as well.
    6. The plan guide. The No Land page and the thank you page promise a plan guide by email, and nothing sends it yet.
    7. November. The reserve by date rolls monthly. Every Design Dollars piece that runs past October 31 needs Mitchell's November date.
    8. The Design Dollars page lists a Rocky Mount Design Center and per studio phone numbers, and its fine print says New Home Specialist. Confirm all three with Mitchell.

    Email

    Every email, by series

    Each email is one complete HTML file in Mitchell's look, with photos hosted on Mitchell's own site and links tagged for tracking. Plain text versions are included.

    To load one into Builder Studio
    1. Marketing, then Emails, then Templates. Create a new template with the code (HTML) editor.
    2. Click Copy HTML here and paste it in. Add the subject line and preview text shown on the card.
    3. Send yourself a test, check the unsubscribe link and the photos, then schedule it to the segment on the card.
    Website

    Home Portrait pop-up and landing page

    The pop-up is one paste in block for mitchellhomesinc.com. It waits fifteen seconds or half a page of scrolling, never shows on quiz pages, and stays away for two weeks once closed. The landing page shows what a visitor sees before they take the quiz.

    Pop-up snippet
